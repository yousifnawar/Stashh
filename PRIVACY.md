# Stash — Privacy Policy

*Last updated: 11 September 2026*

Stash is a clipboard and screenshot history app for macOS. This policy explains what it does with your information. The short version: **it never leaves your Mac.**

## What Stash stores

To show you your history, Stash saves the following **on your Mac only**:

- Text, links, images and file references you copy
- Screenshots from a folder you choose
- Files you place on the Shelf
- The name of the app each item was copied from
- Your settings, categories, tags and keyboard shortcuts

All of it lives inside Stash's own storage on your computer. You can delete any item, clear your whole history, or remove the app and everything with it at any time.

## What Stash does not do

- **No data is collected.** Stash has no servers and no accounts.
- **Nothing is transmitted.** The app contains no networking code. Your clipboard, screenshots and files are never uploaded, synced or shared.
- **No analytics, advertising or tracking** of any kind, and no third-party SDKs.
- **Password managers are ignored.** Stash skips content marked as concealed by password managers and never records it.

## Permissions

- **Screenshot folder** — only if you choose one, so new screenshots appear in your history. You can change or remove it in Settings.
- **Files you drag in** — only the files you drop onto Stash yourself.

## Children

Stash does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children, because it collects no information at all.
