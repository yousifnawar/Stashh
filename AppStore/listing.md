Everything you copy, one hover away. Your clipboard and screenshots live in the notch — search them, drag them, paste them back.

Description:
Your Mac only remembers the last thing you copied. Stash remembers all of it.

Every piece of text, link, colour, image, file and screenshot you copy is saved into a beautiful visual history — and it lives right in your MacBook's notch, the one spot on your screen that's always within reach.

THE NOTCH
Move your pointer to the notch and your recent clips slide out. Click one to copy it and jump back to the app you were in. Prefer the keyboard? Press ⇧⌘P.

QUICK SEARCH
Press ⇧⌘V anywhere, type a few letters, press Return. Fuzzy search finds the clip even when you only half-remember it.

A LIBRARY THAT SORTS ITSELF
Clips are grouped automatically by type — text, links, code, colours, images, screenshots and files — and by the app they came from. Pin favourites, add tags, and create your own categories.

THE SHELF
Drag any file toward the top of the screen and a drop zone appears. Park files there, then drag them wherever they need to go — perfect when the destination isn't open yet.

DRAG, DROP, MOVE MANY
Drag any clip straight into another app. Select several and move them together.

MAKE IT YOURS
Every keyboard shortcut can be changed. Choose how long history is kept. Turn hover-to-open off if you prefer.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• Everything stays on your Mac — Stash has no servers, no accounts and no networking at all
• No tracking, no analytics, no ads
• Content from password managers is never recorded

Works on every Mac running macOS 14 or later. On Macs without a notch, Stash sits neatly at the top centre of your screen.

## App Review Information
Sign-in required:  No

Notes:
Stash is a menu bar utility — it has no Dock icon. On first launch a short tutorial window opens; afterwards the app is reached from its menu bar icon (a dashed square) at the top right of the screen.

To test:
1. Copy any text in any app (e.g. select text in Safari and press ⌘C).
2. Press ⇧⌘P, or move the pointer onto the notch at the top centre of the screen. The copied item appears in the strip. On Macs without a notch a small "Stash" handle is shown there instead.
3. Press ⇧⌘V to open Quick Search, type part of the copied text, press Return. The item is copied and focus returns to the previous app, where ⌘V pastes it. Stash does not simulate keystrokes.
4. Press ⇧⌘C to open the full Library window.
5. Shelf: drag any file from Finder toward the top of the screen; a drop zone appears under the notch. Release to park the file; it appears under the Shelf tab and can be dragged back out.

Screenshots are only captured from a folder the user explicitly chooses in Settings (security-scoped bookmark). The app contains no networking code and collects no data.

## App Sandbox Information (click + and add each one)

com.apple.security.files.user-selected.read-write
  The user chooses the folder their macOS screenshots are saved to. Stash watches
  only that folder so new screenshots appear in their clipboard history. No other
  location is read or written.

com.apple.security.files.bookmarks.app-scope
  A security-scoped bookmark stores the folder the user chose, so it can be
  reopened on the next launch without asking them to pick it again.
